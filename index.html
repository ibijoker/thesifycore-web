Thesis journey

From synopsis to defense

The path a thesis takes with our dedicated team, one stage at a time. Scroll to move along it.

1of 7

Synopsis

  1. Synopsis

    Shape the topic, research gap and objectives into a clear synopsis your department can approve.

  2. Literature review

    Map existing work, find what is missing and place your study inside the field.

  3. Methodology

    Design the approach, data collection and tools so the study can be repeated and defended.

  4. Analysis

    Run the analysis, check the results and turn them into findings that answer the research question.

  5. Chapter writing

    Draft each chapter in one consistent academic voice, from introduction to conclusion.

  6. Formatting & citations

    Apply your university template and reference style, including APA, IEEE, MLA and Harvard.

  7. Feedback & defense prep

    Align the draft with supervisor feedback and prepare slides and answers for the defense.
